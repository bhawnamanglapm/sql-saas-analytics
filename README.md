# SQL Analytics on a fictional SaaS subscription business (Basic and Advanced)

**SQL practice project on a synthetic dataset.**

## In one minute

A fictional B2B SaaS company ('Aptura') generated by a Python script: 900 customers, three plans and 24 months of subscription history, with churn modeled on two patterns (cheaper plans churn more; paid-search customers churn more than referral customers). The page says the author installed SQL Server 2022 Express and ran 8 basic queries (SELECT, JOIN, GROUP BY, CASE) and 4 advanced ones (CTEs, LAG, SUM() OVER, RANK() PARTITION BY, monthly cohort retention), and hit and fixed a T-SQL window-frame error.
